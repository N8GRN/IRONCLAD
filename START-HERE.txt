IRONCLAD CRM
============

This zip is the whole app. Unzip it and you should see index.html
in the same folder as js/, css/, and brand/.

SIGN-IN
- One login: email + password. Create account / Forgot password are
  on that screen.
- New accounts wait until Nate or Matt grant Sales, Manager, or Admin.
- Roster is Firestore `users` only — no example names in the app.
- Everyone shares the same company jobs (ironclad-127a5 / users).
- After you sign in once on an iPad, Continue offline uses YOUR
  account only — no picking someone else’s name.
- Alerts go to the project owner you assigned, not to yourself.

TEAM & PERMISSIONS
Settings → Team: real accounts only. Phone, commission % per person.
Settings → Manage permissions: Admins decide what Manager and Sales
can see/edit (Materials, Labor, Team). Managers start view-only on
those three. Sales starts with none. Admins always have full access.

JOB TABS
Overview · Assessment · Summary · Contract
Summary opens Customer quote, Job Sheet (materials only), and Job cost.

ASSESSMENT / TAKEOFF (this build)
- Facets on each structure (squares, pitch, tear-off). Stories stay
  on the structure. Customer Price $/square is billed labor.
- Linears (eaves, rakes, ridge, hips, valleys) are per structure.
- 12% waste on materials except drip (rakes, 10% + 1 stick),
  gutter apron (eaves, 10%), felt (no waste), and ridge vent (no waste).
- Felt: 10 sq/roll, 4/12+ only, no waste.
- Ice & Water: 4/12+ eaves+valleys+½ wall flashing at 60 lf/roll;
  2/12–3.9/12 is ½ roll/sq and no felt.
- Flat Roof pitch: Base sheet + MuleHide cap + custom edge metal
  ($1.03/lf). No shingles/felt/ice/starter/drip/hip on an all-flat
  structure.
- Vent: Ridge vents (ridge lf ÷ 30 lf/roll, no waste) OR box vents.
- Chimneys: count × $500 (editable in Estimate defaults).
- Step flashing: ceil((wall flashing lf + chimneys × 10) / 50) bundles.
- Broan: 4" bath and 8" kitchen as separate counts.
- Sheathing is a sheet count (10 = 10 sheets of OSB).
- Sales tax: 7% on materials, between commission and Other.
  Rate is editable on Assessment and in Estimate defaults.
- Proposal profit uses actual crew Labor & tear-off (same as Job cost).

MATERIALS
Sidebar Materials (if your role can read it). Add colors, retire SKUs,
bump prices. Ridge vent SkyRunner LTE $88/roll; box vents $18.

ALERTS
Each person: Alerts → Notifications. Defaults:
  on  — assigned to me, status of my job
  on for admins/managers — a new job is created
  off — scheduled, complete
Tap Enable push once per iPad (Allow Notifications; iPhone/iPad:
Add to Home Screen first). Cache ironclad-crm-v6 — if a Home Screen
icon looks old, remove it and add the site again.

LABOR
Sidebar → Labor. Per crew: install pay $/sq for each pitch, tear-off
1–5 layer, OSB $/sheet, wood $/board. Actual cost = measured squares
at each pitch × that pitch’s crew rate × stories. Assessment Price
$/square is what the customer is charged. Job cost uses crew pay.

QUOTED PRICE
On Assessment, optional Quoted price. Lower than list → customer
Estimate shows the % discount. Higher is hidden from the customer.
